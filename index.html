How it works

Three steps. No deposit, no checkout.

From the sofa you like to the sofa standing in your living room, placed by our own team — without paying a penny up front.

01

Choose your sofa

Browse the collection and pick the piece you want. Every sofa can be made in your choice of fabric, leather or colour, and to your own measurements.

02

Confirm it on WhatsApp

Tap order and send us your details. We confirm the size, fabric, colour and a delivery window that suits you. No payment, no deposit, no card details.

03

We bring it in and place it

Within 24–72 hours our team carries it into your home, assembles it and places it exactly where you want it, then clears the packaging away. You settle in cash once you are happy.
